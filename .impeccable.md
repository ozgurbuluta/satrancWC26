# Design context

The design direction for this site, so that later work can be checked against
it. Confirmed by Özgür on 25 November 2026 with a reference image for the
main view; recorded in issue 7. It replaces the earlier dark green look.

## Who it is for

Turkish chess fans following the 2026 World Championship match, usually with
the Turkish broadcast open next to the board, on a laptop or a phone. They
want to see the position, understand who is better, and try their own ideas
without losing the live game.

## Character

A calm chess workspace. Warm, light, unhurried. The board is the main thing
on the page; everything else is quieter than it.

## The main view

- **Top bar.** The site's own name on the left. In the middle the match at a
  glance: both players, the score, and one small square per game that opens
  it (played, in progress, to come). The view switch and the status on the
  right. The full list of games, with players, results and dates, is one
  button away at every width.
- **Left of the board.** What the engine says about the position on the
  board: win chance as three numbers and a bar, then the engine's lines, then
  the time each move took.
- **The board.** As large as the height allows. A player bar above and below
  with the clock; the evaluation as one bar above the board; under it one
  strip with the moves, the steps and the way back to the live position.
- **Right of the board.** The broadcast, chosen from a dropdown, and the tabs
  (moves, prediction, similar games, charts, match, settings).
- **Phone and tablet.** One column. The video stays on top, the board keeps
  the full width, and the engine and time panels move into the tabs. Touch
  targets are at least 40 px, 44 px for the board controls.

## Palette

Warm cream surfaces, dark brown text, restrained accents. All in
`src/styles.css` as tokens on `:root`.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#f1e9dd` | page |
| `--panel` | `#faf6ef` | cards |
| `--panel-2`, `--panel-3` | `#efe6d8`, `#e5d9c6` | quiet fills, hover |
| `--line`, `--line-strong` | `#dfd3c0`, `#b9ad9a` | borders |
| `--text`, `--muted` | `#2e2620`, `#645a4e` | text |
| `--accent` | `#4f7a34` | the active choice, the engine's arrow |
| `--danger` | `#c0392f` | live, return to live |
| `--amber` | `#b9791f` | the viewer's own line, pause |
| `--player-a`, `--player-b` | `#3b2f26`, `#b0794a` | the two players, the same from game to game |
| `--light-sq`, `--dark-sq` | `#f0d9b5`, `#b58863` | the board |

Text on every surface is meant to meet WCAG AA (4.5:1 for small text). Colour
is never the only signal: live has a dot and a word, results have text
labels, the two players are named beside their bars.

## Type

The system font stack. Section labels are small, spaced capitals. Numbers
that change use tabular figures. Player names inside Turkish text are marked
`lang="en"` where they are capitalised, so "Sindarov" does not become
"SİNDAROV".

## Engine lines

Each line is a row: a score badge, the move the engine would play in strong
type, the rest of the line quieter, two lines at most. Rows are separated by
a hairline and have room to breathe.

## Time per move

One row per move, one player's bars to the left and the other's to the
right. Each player keeps their side and colour from game to game. A scale
under the chart, move numbers down the middle, the move on the board
highlighted, and each player's longest think under it. Only real clock data
is drawn. A move whose time is not known gets a small hollow mark and is
counted in a note; it is never drawn as a bar of zero.

## Motion

Pieces move only when a move is made or stepped through, one move at a time.
A jump is shown at once. Nothing slides or fades in when it appears, and
nothing on the page shifts when a line of the viewer's own is started. The
few transitions that exist (the evaluation bar, the win chance bar) are
switched off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Not this

- No branding, wording or assets taken from the reference image or from any
  other site. The reference set the layout and the mood, nothing else.
- No generated commentary and no chat. The site shows what the feed, the
  engine and the databases actually say.
- No invented numbers: no probabilities of a player's move, no estimated
  times, no made-up results.
- No left-border callout stripes, no " · " separators in meta lines, no
  filler reassurance sentences.

## Words

Turkish first. "Oyun", not "parti". "Sen", never "siz". No circumflex. No em
dashes in Turkish copy.
